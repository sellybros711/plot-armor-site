Common questions

Is what I read on X sent anywhere?

No. Every Plot Armor extension decides what to cover inside your browser, on your computer. There's no Plot Armor account or server. Each privacy policy lists exactly what that extension downloads or asks for.

A spoiler got through. What now?

Use Report a problem for Gaming or for Movies & TV, and tell us the game or show and roughly what the post said. "Show anyway" only uncovers one post, so it won't cause this.

Does it work on my phone, or in the X app?

Not yet. Plot Armor extensions work in your browser on a computer (Chrome or Edge), on x.com.

How do I remove an extension?

Right-click the Plot Armor icon in your browser's toolbar and choose the "Remove" option. Everything it saved is deleted with it.

Is Plot Armor part of X?

No. Plot Armor is not affiliated with X, or with any show, network, league, team or game company.
