Plot Armor: Sports Spoiler Blocker

Recorded the game? Watching it later? Plot Armor hides the result on X until you've seen it, and leaves everything else alone.

Notify me when it's out

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store. "Notify me" opens your email app with a short message to us; we use your address only to tell you when it's out. No email app? Write to PlotArmorGG@outlook.com with "Notify me: Sports".

How it works

  1. Find your game

    Open Plot Armor and find it in today's schedule, in your own time zone.

  2. Tap "Hide results"

    From kickoff on, posts about that game get a "Possible spoiler" cover. Every other game stays visible.

  3. Watched it?

    Tap "I've watched it" and the covers lift.

The schedule, built in

  • NFL, NBA, NHL and MLB
  • Soccer: Premier League, Champions League and MLS
  • Today's games, the last two days (for the game you recorded last night) and the week ahead
  • Filter by league or search for your team
  • No scores, ever: the schedule only lists who plays and when

What it covers for your game

  • Posts that name either team, their players or coaches, fan names and game hashtags
  • The two teams' official accounts, and league and sports-news accounts when they post about either team
  • Quote posts, replies under a covered post, link previews and photo descriptions
  • The "What's happening" and "Today's News" boxes, from kickoff until you've watched
  • Everyday words like "Heat" or "Bills" on their own don't trigger a cover
  • Games stop being hidden on their own 3 days after kickoff; "Keep hiding" adds a day

Private by design

  • Everything happens on your computer. No account, no sign-in, no Plot Armor server.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no ads. What you read on X is never sent anywhere.
  • Its only connection to the internet is downloading the game schedule (who plays and when).
