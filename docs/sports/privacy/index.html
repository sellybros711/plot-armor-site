Plot Armor: Sports Spoiler Blocker privacy policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Plot Armor: Sports Spoiler Blocker works entirely on your computer. It does not collect, sell or share your data, and it does not send anything you read or do to anyone.

What Plot Armor reads

Plot Armor only runs on x.com and twitter.com. On those pages it reads the posts on your screen (their words, date, author name, link previews and photo descriptions) and the trending topics, so it can decide whether to cover each one. This happens inside your browser, on your computer. Post text is never saved and never sent anywhere.

What Plot Armor saves, and where

Everything is saved in your browser's own storage on your computer:

  • the games you chose to hide, and until when;
  • your settings (how covers look, the league you last picked in the schedule);
  • a copy of the game schedule (teams, start times and team names);
  • words Plot Armor learned automatically from posts about a game you are hiding (only the words themselves, never the posts), which expire after 48 hours;
  • how many posts it covered this week and last week (just the number, never which posts), shown in the popup.

There is no account, no sign-in and no Plot Armor server. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

What Plot Armor downloads

When your browser starts and every 3 hours, Plot Armor downloads the latest game schedule (who plays whom, start times, and team and player names) from sellybros711.github.io/plot-armor-sports-data, a public website hosted by GitHub. This is a plain download, like opening a web page: nothing about you or the games you hide is sent. As with any website, GitHub can see your IP address when the file is downloaded. See GitHub's privacy statement.

This is the only connection Plot Armor makes to the internet.

What Plot Armor does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of data, and no use of data for anything except covering spoilers.
  • No reading of other websites, your messages, or your X account details.
  • No posting, liking or anything else on X on your behalf.
  • No scores or results: the schedule it downloads lists only who plays and when.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: PlotArmorGG@outlook.com.

Plot Armor is not affiliated with X, or with any league, team, broadcaster or sports-news outlet.
