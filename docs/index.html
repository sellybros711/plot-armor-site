Questions

How do I block spoilers on X (Twitter)?

Add a Plot Armor extension to your browser, pick the game or show you want protected, and keep scrolling. Posts about it are covered until you say you've caught up.

Does it work on Twitter?

Yes. X is the new name for Twitter. Plot Armor works on x.com in your browser on a computer: Chrome or Edge.

Is Plot Armor free?

Yes. Plot Armor: Gaming and Plot Armor: Spoiler Blocker for Movies & TV are free.

Where can I get Filter and Sports?

Filter is out now for Microsoft Edge. Sports is on the way: press Notify me and we'll email you when it's out.
