Questions

More help is on the support page.

How do I block spoilers on X (Twitter)?

Add a Plot Armor extension to Chrome, pick the game or show you want protected, and keep scrolling. Posts about it are covered until you say you've caught up.

Does it work on Twitter?

Yes. X is the new name for Twitter. Plot Armor works on x.com in Chrome on a computer.

Is Plot Armor free?

Yes. Plot Armor: Gaming and Plot Armor: Spoiler Blocker for Movies & TV are free in the Chrome Web Store.

Can Plot Armor see what I read?

No. Each extension decides what to cover inside your browser. There's no account and no Plot Armor server, so what you read on X is never sent to us.

When are Filter and Sports coming?

They're being built. Press Notify me on either one and we'll email you when it's in the Chrome Web Store.

How do I remove an extension?

Right-click its icon in Chrome and choose Remove from Chrome. Removing it deletes everything it saved.
