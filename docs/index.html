Private by design

  • No account and no sign-in. There is no Plot Armor server.
  • Every extension decides what to cover inside your browser. What you read on X is never sent to us.
  • No analytics, tracking or ads, and no cookies of our own, in the extensions or on this website.
  • Removing an extension deletes everything it saved.

Some extensions download public lists (show dates, game schedules) so they know what to cover. Each privacy policy says exactly what each one downloads or asks for.
