How it works

  1. Pick a game

    Open Plot Armor and press "Hide content" on a game you're playing or waiting for.

  2. Posts get covered

    Every post about it gets a "Possible spoiler" cover: story, endings, boss fights, leaks and trailers.

  3. Finished it?

    Press "I've finished it" and everything comes back.

What it covers

  • Posts that name the game, its characters, places or hashtags
  • The game's official accounts, and replies to them
  • Search and hashtag pages for the game
  • Quote posts, replies under a covered post, link previews and photo descriptions
  • Trending topics about the game
  • Everyday words like "GTA" or "Jason" only count when the post is clearly about the game
  • "Show anyway" uncovers one post; pick a thin bar or a full blur

Private by design

  • Everything happens on your computer. No account, no sign-in, no Plot Armor server.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no ads. What you read on X is never sent anywhere.
  • Its only connection to the internet is downloading the game lists (names and keywords).
