Plot Armor: Gaming

Covers video-game story spoilers on X until you've finished the game. First up: GTA 6.

Notify me when it's out

Being built. "Notify me" opens your email app with a short message to us; we use your address only to tell you when it's out. No email app? Write to PlotArmorGG@outlook.com with "Notify me: Gaming".

The idea

  • Add a game you're playing, or about to play
  • Posts about it get a "Possible spoiler" cover: story, endings, missions, leaks and trailers
  • "Show anyway" uncovers one post; "I've finished it" stops hiding that game

Plot Armor: Gaming is still being built, so details may change before it reaches the Chrome Web Store. Like every Plot Armor extension, it will work on your computer with no account and no Plot Armor server.
