Plot Armor: Gaming privacy policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

Plot Armor: Gaming works entirely on your computer. It does not collect, sell or share your data, and it does not send anything you read or do to anyone.

What Plot Armor reads

Plot Armor only runs on x.com and twitter.com. On those pages it reads the posts on your screen (their words, date, author name, link previews and photo descriptions), the trending topics, and the search words on X's search and hashtag pages, so it can decide whether each one is about a game you are hiding. This happens inside your browser, on your computer. Post text is never saved and never sent anywhere.

What Plot Armor saves, and where

Everything is saved in your browser's own storage on your computer:

  • the games you are hiding, and which ones you have marked finished;
  • how covers look (thin bar or full blur);
  • a copy of the game lists (names, keywords and release dates).

There is no account, no sign-in and no Plot Armor server. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

What Plot Armor downloads

When your browser starts and once a day, Plot Armor downloads the latest game lists (game names, character names, keywords and release dates) from sellybros711.github.io/plot-armor-games-data, a public website hosted by GitHub. This is a plain download, like opening a web page: nothing about you or your games is sent. As with any website, GitHub can see your IP address when the files are downloaded. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Apart from the feedback forms below, which only open if you click or remove Plot Armor, this is the only connection Plot Armor makes to the internet. It never uploads anything.

Reporting a problem, and removing Plot Armor

The "Report a problem" link in Plot Armor opens a form on Google Forms in a new tab, and when you remove Plot Armor, Chrome opens a short "Why did you remove it?" form. Plot Armor itself sends nothing; you choose what to type and whether to submit it. We use what you send only to decide what to fix and which games to add, and, if you leave an email address, to reply to you. We do not share it or use it for anything else. Google's privacy policy covers the form itself. To have something you sent deleted, email us at the address below.

What Plot Armor does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of data, and no use of data for anything except covering spoilers.
  • No reading of other websites, your messages, or your X account details.
  • No posting, liking or anything else on X on your behalf.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: PlotArmorGG@outlook.com.

Plot Armor is not affiliated with X, or with any game, studio or publisher it covers.
