Plot Armor: Spoiler Blocker for Movies & TV

Watch it your way. Covers spoilers on X for the shows, movies and wrestling you haven't seen yet.

Add to ChromeRequest a show

How it works

  1. Add a show

    Pick it in the Plot Armor popup.

  2. Say where you are

    Tell Plot Armor the last episode you've watched. Any post about something newer gets a "Possible spoiler" cover.

  3. Tick it when you've watched

    The covers lift on their own. Starting from the beginning? Choose "Hide everything".

What it covers

  • WWE: RAW, SmackDown, NXT and premium live events, each tracked separately
  • AEW: Dynamite, Collision and pay-per-views
  • Survivor, The Amazing Race, Big Brother, The Traitors, and more on the way
  • Movies too: hide trailers, set photos and leaks for a big release (starting with Avengers: Doomsday) until you've seen it
  • Posts, quote posts, replies under a covered post, link previews and photo descriptions
  • The "What's happening" trends box, covered completely on the night an episode airs

Built for live TV

  • Names and hashtags that suddenly trend on an episode night are picked up while you browse
  • "Watching live tonight" shows everything for the night and switches itself off at 4 AM
  • Protect a big event, like a pay-per-view or a finale, with one tap before it starts
  • "Did you watch it?" reminds you after an episode airs, so covers never pile up
  • "Show anyway" uncovers a single post; "I've watched this" right on the cover marks it watched
  • A thin bar or a full blur: you pick how covered posts look

Private by design

  • Everything happens on your computer. No account, no sign-in, no Plot Armor server.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no ads. What you read on X is never sent anywhere.
  • Its only connection to the internet is downloading the show lists (episode dates, cast names and keywords).
