Plot Armor: Filter Out What You Don't Want

Hides gambling, crypto spam, scams and ads on X. Switch on what you never want to see, and it stays out of your feed.

Notify me when it's out

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store. "Notify me" opens your email app with a short message to us; we use your address only to tell you when it's out. No email app? Write to RunTheGames@outlook.com with "Notify me: Filter".

What it hides

Gambling

Sportsbooks, prediction markets, betting tipsters and promo-code spam, including posts from gambling companies' own accounts.

Spam & scams

Crypto shilling and pasted coin addresses, bot-like accounts and copy-paste replies.

Ads

X's promoted posts.

Each category is simply on or off. More categories are on the way.

Kind, not preachy

  • Hidden posts get a thin bar or a full blur that says why: "Hidden: Gambling"
  • "Show anyway" uncovers one post. For gambling it asks once more first, to give you a moment
  • Spots fancy 𝗯𝗼𝗹𝗱 letters, betting words in names and bios, and accounts it has seen before

Where accounts are based (optional)

Off until you switch it on. When on, Plot Armor Filter asks X for the "About this account" details of accounts on your screen or that you recently scrolled past, the same way X's own page does and using your X sign-in, so it can show where an account is based and hide places you choose. Only the account's @handle is asked about, at most 40 every 15 minutes, and the answers are kept on your computer.

Private by design

  • Everything is decided on your computer. No account with us, no Plot Armor server.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no ads. What you read on X is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Its word lists are built in. The only requests it makes are the optional account lookups, which go only to X.
